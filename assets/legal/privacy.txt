1. What stays on your device
Favorites, recently played sounds, saved mixes, settings and downloaded audio are stored only on your device. LunaNest has no user accounts.

2. Data we collect
LunaNest itself does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, microphone or health data, and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

3. Subscriptions
If you buy Premium, the purchase is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. LunaNest uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) to verify your subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, purchase/subscription status and device/store information needed to provide entitlement checks. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. Network requests
Streamed or downloaded sounds are fetched over HTTPS from [AUDIO HOST]. The host may see your IP address and request metadata, as with any web request.

5. Your choices
Settings → "Erase all local data" removes everything stored by LunaNest on your device. You can cancel subscriptions in your store account settings.
